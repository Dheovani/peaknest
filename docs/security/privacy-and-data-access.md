# Privacy and data access

Fitness and nutrition records are sensitive personal data in the Brazilian LGPD launch context. The foundation contains no personal health fixtures or production records. Local PostgreSQL credentials in `.env.example` are development placeholders only.

Before protected routes launch, document processing purpose and legal basis with counsel, collect explicit granular collaborator grants, enforce expiry/revocation immediately, and record audit metadata without health payloads. Data export and rights requests must separate the subject's data from third-party private data and preserve legally required records. Retention periods require legal review and will be recorded in `docs/security/data-retention.md` before production.
