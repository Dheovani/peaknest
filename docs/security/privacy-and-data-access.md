# Privacy and data access

Fitness and nutrition records are sensitive personal data in the Brazilian LGPD launch context. The first web identity slice stores personal names, email addresses, password hashes, and browser sessions in PostgreSQL. It contains no personal health fixtures or production records. Local PostgreSQL credentials in `.env.example` are development placeholders only. Better Auth owns session cookies and password hashing; the application never logs submitted passwords. A personal signup grants no professional credential or access to another person's data.

Before protected health-resource routes launch, document processing purpose and legal basis with counsel, collect explicit granular collaborator grants, enforce expiry/revocation immediately, and record audit metadata without health payloads. Data export and rights requests must separate the subject's data from third-party private data and preserve legally required records. Retention periods require legal review and will be recorded in `docs/security/data-retention.md` before production. Email verification, account recovery, and shared rate-limit storage are required before production account access.
